# Results

All numbers are produced from the files in `results/` by `src/make_tables.py`. The paper's tables
(`paper/tab_*.tex`) and in-text numbers (`paper/numbers.tex`) are generated by the same script.
Test set: 3 unseen scenes, 150 database images and 1,050 queries each (3,150 queries in total). A query
is correct if a retrieved database image lies within 10 m and 60° of heading.

## Stage-2 re-ranking (top-10 Galatea seed-0 candidates, test)

`Local` sorts candidates by the local score only. `Fused` uses s = s_g + β·s_ℓ/(s_ℓ+κ), with β and κ tuned
on the validation scene.

| Local scorer | Local R@1 | Fused R@1 | Fused R@5 | β | κ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MNN | 59.2 | 66.0 | 89.5 | 1 | 80 |
| MNN + MAGSAC++ | 62.4 | 67.2 | 90.0 | 8 | 640 |
| OT, appearance only | 57.0 | 65.8 | 90.4 | 0.5 | 20 |
| OT + MAGSAC++ | 61.0 | 63.0 | 88.6 | 0.5 | 20 |
| LightGlue | 57.5 | 65.3 | 89.6 | 1 | 160 |
| LightGlue + MAGSAC++ | 60.0 | 65.7 | 89.8 | 4 | 640 |
| Partial FGW (ours) | 59.8 | 66.0 | 90.1 | 1 | 80 |

Global ranking alone gives R@1 60.8 / R@5 88.0. Across the three stage-1 seeds, fusion adds +6.3 ± 0.8
(FGW), +6.9 ± 0.4 (MNN + MAGSAC++) and +5.2 ± 0.6 (MNN) points of R@1.

## Dust-bin analysis (test images, seed-0 heads)

- SALAD (balanced): 83.3% of token mass goes to the dust-bin. Its correlation with the sky fraction is ρ = 0.63, and the coefficient of variation of cluster mass is 0.18.
- Galatea (λ ≈ 0.5): 0.4% goes to the dust-bin, and the coefficient of variation of cluster mass is 6.4. Retrieval accuracy is identical, as Proposition 4 predicts.
