\subsection{Terrestrial models under lunar illumination change}\label{sec:zeroshot}
\Cref{tab:main} (top) shows that all three terrestrial models perform reasonably when only the
viewpoint changes: at C0, R@1 is $\zsEigCzero$--$\zsCosCzero\%$. They degrade sharply as soon as the
Sun moves. DINOv2-SALAD, the strongest of them, falls from $\zsSaladCzero\%$ at C0 to
$\zsSaladCtwo\%$ under grazing illumination (C2) and to $\zsSaladCfive\%$ when the shadows flip (C5).
The CNN-based CosPlace and EigenPlaces fare worse, with C5 values of $\zsCosCfive\%$ and $\zsEigCfive\%$.
Even a foundation-model backbone pre-trained on 142M images therefore does not provide
illumination-invariant place descriptors on regolith. Local re-ranking does not close the gap
either. Fusing MNN+MAGSAC++ matches into the top-10 zero-shot SALAD candidates (selected on
validation as in \cref{sec:rerank}) raises R@1 to $\zsRRRone\%$. That is still far below any
lunar-trained stage~1 \emph{without} re-ranking, and only $\zsRRHard\%$ on the hardest conditions,
because the correct place is often not among the candidates at all.

\subsection{Global--local fusion}\label{sec:rerank}
\Cref{tab:rerank} re-ranks the top-10 candidates of the frozen Galatea head (seed 0, R@1
$\rrGlobRone\%$) with four local scorers. Two findings stand out. First, ranking by the local score
alone barely helps and can hurt: no scorer improves R@1 by more than about two points, and several
lose ground. Second, fusing local and global evidence with \cref{eq:fusion} helps every scorer. With
partial FGW, R@1 rises to $\rrFgwRone\%$, a gain of $\rrGainMean$ points (95\% CI
$[\rrGainLo,\rrGainHi]$), and on the hardest conditions from $\galSzeroHard\%$ to $\rrFgwHard\%$, the
best of all scorers on that subset. Differences between scorers in the fused setting are small and
within the confidence intervals: FGW vs.\ LightGlue $\fgwVsLgMean$ $[\fgwVsLgLo,\fgwVsLgHi]$, and FGW
vs.\ appearance-only OT $\fgwVsUotMean$ $[\fgwVsUotLo,\fgwVsUotHi]$. The training-free structural
matcher thus performs as well as a learned terrestrial matcher. MAGSAC++ helps MNN and LightGlue but
not the OT matchers, whose dust-bins already reject outliers.

\paragraph*{Why local-only re-ranking fails and fusion works}
\Cref{tab:rescue} splits the effect of re-ranking into queries it \emph{rescues} (wrong global top-1,
correct after re-ranking) and queries it \emph{breaks} (the reverse). Local-only ranking rescues many
queries, $\rbFrFgwLocRes\%$ with FGW, but breaks as many ($\rbFrFgwLocBrk\%$): SuperPoint responses on
regolith are dominated by shadow edges, so a wrong candidate photographed under similar light can
collect more matches than the right one. The overrides that break a correct result are those against
a confident global ranking: the median global similarity margin they overturn is $\marginBroken$,
twice that of the overrides that rescue a query ($\marginRescued$). This is the case that
\cref{prop:override} rules out. Fusion keeps about half of the rescues ($\rbFrFgwFusRes\%$) while
breaking only $\rbFrFgwFusBrk\%$, and the same pattern holds for every scorer and for the fine-tuned
stage~1, where local-only ranking breaks up to $\rbFtLgLocBrk\%$ of the queries and fusion at most
$\rbFtMnnRFusBrk\%$.

\paragraph*{Robustness across stage-1 models}
Repeating the protocol on all three seeds of each stage-1 model, and re-tuning $\beta,\kappa$ on
validation each time, fusion with FGW adds $\msFgwGain\pm\msFgwGainsd$ points to the frozen Galatea
head (MNN+MAGSAC++: $\msMnnRGain\pm\msMnnRGainsd$; plain MNN: $\msMnnGain\pm\msMnnGainsd$),
$\ftFgwGain\pm\ftFgwGainsd$ to the fine-tuned Galatea head (MNN+MAGSAC++: $\ftMnnRGain\pm\ftMnnRGainsd$)
and $\msnvFgwGain\pm\msnvFgwGainsd$ to the fine-tuned NetVLAD head (MNN+MAGSAC++:
$\msnvMnnRGain\pm\msnvMnnRGainsd$). The gain shrinks as stage~1 improves, because fewer queries remain
to be rescued, but it never vanishes. The complete system, fine-tuned NetVLAD with FGW fusion, reaches
$\msnvFgwRone\pm\msnvFgwRonesd\%$ R@1 and $\msnvFgwRfive\%$ R@5 ($\msnvFgwHard\%$ on the hardest
conditions), against $\zsSaladRone\%$ for the best terrestrial model. On the Moon, \emph{how local and
global evidence are combined matters more than which local matcher is used}.
