\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}
% ======================================================================
On LunarSim-VPR, lunar training ($+\gainZsMean$ points over zero-shot) and cluster-based aggregation
($>20$ points over global pooling) are prerequisites, assumed below.

\paragraph*{1) Training data}
Illumination diversity in training is worth $\fixIllDrop$ points of R@1 for the reference head with a
frozen backbone and $\ftFixIllDrop$ with a fine-tuned one, more than fine-tuning adds to the same head.

\paragraph*{2) Backbone adaptation}
Fine-tuning four blocks adds $11$--$19$ points to every head in LunarSim, a gain absent on real panoramas and
in the second simulator (\cref{sec:real,sec:lunarloc}).

\paragraph*{3) Aggregation}
Residual NetVLAD aggregation differs from its closest OT relative by $\famFrNvVsNDMean$ points on frozen
tokens and by $\famFtNvVsNDMean$ once the backbone is trained, more than the assignment changes.

\paragraph*{4) Assignment}
Within the OT family all values of $\lambda$ are equivalent on a frozen backbone (P1, P2), the fixed members
stay within $\famFtLamRange$ points of each other after fine-tuning, and training cannot choose among them
(\cref{sec:family,sec:ft}); $\lambda=0$ needs no Sinkhorn loop.

\paragraph*{5) Re-ranking}
Shadow edges dominate keypoint detections, so local evidence must grow with the global margin to
overturn a global decision (\cref{prop:override}). Fusion is the one result that holds on real panoramas ($+\apGainLo$ to $+\apGainHi$ points). CLIPPER is the
best fused scorer in LunarSim (with MAGSAC++) and gains most under the second simulator's lighting change. Retrieval does not yet give a metric pose: PnP on LightGlue
matches with exact database depth is within $1$\,m and $5^\circ$ for $\poLgSucc\%$ of queries
($\poLgSuccCzero\%$ under the database Sun, $\poLgSuccHard\%$ under the hardest conditions).

\paragraph*{6) Theory}
Predictions (P1)--(P3) held for fixed tokens with a dust-bin; the ordering of heads with trained tokens is
empirical, and on real photographs (\cref{sec:real}) only the weakness of GeM is confirmed.
