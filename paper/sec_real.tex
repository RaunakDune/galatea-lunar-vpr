We ran two station-level tests on Apollo Hasselblad surface
photographs~\cite{asuapollo} (\cref{tab:real}; protocols in the supplement). \emph{Panoramas}: the
Apollo Lunar Surface Journal~\cite{alsj} lists the frames of each station pan. We query each
frame of $79$ pans ($47$ stations) against the other pans of its mission, which differ in photographer,
standpoint, heading and usually Sun elevation ($\reNq$ queries at $\reNst$ stations of Apollo~15--17). \emph{Rendered map}: we rendered $12$ headings at $\rrNst$ Apollo~15 stations from the $2$\,m LROC
NAC elevation model~\cite{robinson2010lroc} under the Sun of each station, with DESPINA~\cite{nelsonarcher2026despina}
and as shaded renders; $\rrNq$ frames from $\rrNpans$ pans are the queries.

\paragraph*{Re-ranking}
We re-ranked each head's top~10 on the panoramas with the parameters tuned on LunarSim. Fusion adds
$\apGainLo$--$\apGainHi$ points for every head and scorer, all intervals above zero; tuned on the other two
missions instead, it adds $\apLomoLo$--$\apLomoHi$. The scorers lie within $\apScorerSpread$ points of each other, and unlike in simulation the
local score alone also helps ($+\apLocGainLo$ to $+\apLocGainHi$). Boulder segments in the style of LunarLoc,
matched with CLIPPER, change R@1 by $\apBoulderLo$ to $+\apBoulderHi$ points.
